How to play

Your turn

  • Place two tiles on your board.
  • Player 2 may place three tiles on their first turn only.
  • Then break one connected formation of at least two matching elements, or end your turn. If you have no legal formation, your turn ends automatically.
  • You lose if you cannot place the required tiles (three on Player 2's first turn, two on every other turn) because you do not have enough tiles or empty spaces.
  • A placement cannot create a connected group larger than five tiles.

Formations and actions

  • Tiles connect orthogonally (up, down, left, or right). Sora (空) can join a formation as a wildcard, but it does not connect different element types to each other.
  • Effect actions = formation size minus one, plus your Sora (空) bonus shown beside your name. Sora (空) tiles in the formation count toward its size.
  • Each Sora (空) tile on your board gives your opponent one extra action whenever they break a formation. Sora (空) stays on the board after a formation breaks and cannot be used for attacks.

Element effects

  • Fire (火): Place upside-down tiles from your opponent's piles on their board, up to your action count. You cannot place them on face-up tiles, but you can stack them on upside-down tiles.
  • Water (水): Move one tile one orthogonal space per action. Face-up tiles can move only to empty spaces. You can move an upside-down tile from a stack onto another upside-down tile or off the stack.
  • Earth (地): Flip up to one adjacent upside-down tile per action. Only the top tile of a stack can be flipped; you may end the effect early.
  • Air (風): Choose Fire (火), Water (水), or Earth (地) and use it for your action count. Empty piles can still be chosen, but their tiles cannot be placed.

Attack tiles

  • Upside-down tiles show the board owner's color. They can be stacked on other upside-down tiles, but not on face-up tiles.
